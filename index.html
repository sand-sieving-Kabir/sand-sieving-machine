Project Overview & Background

The Problem Statement

Traditional manual sand sieving using handheld wire mesh screens is extremely labor-intensive, slow, and induces severe physical fatigue (such as lower back strain and muscular stress) among construction workers. Furthermore, it fails to meet large-scale project demands.

The Engineering Solution

This project presents a motorized mechanical screening device fully designed via SolidWorks. Utilizing an electric motor, V-belt drive transmission, and an inclined rotating drum sieve, it efficiently separates fine aggregates from coarse debris continuously.

Motor Selection & Design Suitability

Power & Torque

Electric motor and pulleys are configured to overcome aggregate friction and rotation resistance across a 600 mm diameter drum.

Why Most Suitable?

Rotary trommel design offers continuous feeding, self-clearing action, and compact structure (1040 mm length) ideal for site mobility.

Speed Reduction

V-belt drive reduces high motor RPM to optimum drum rotation speed, preventing aggregate clogging and ensuring smooth throughput.

Advantages & Limitations

Key Advantages

  • High Throughput: Screens significantly larger volumes of sand per hour compared to manual methods.
  • Ergonomic Relief: Greatly eliminates physical strain, back injuries, and fatigue for workers.
  • Consistent Grading: Uniform separation of fine sand from stones and debris using structured mesh.
  • Cost-Effective: Fabricated using accessible mild steel profiles ensuring long-term durability.

Operational Limitations

  • Power Dependency: Requires an external electric power source or generator supply on-site.
  • Moisture Sensitivity: Excessively wet or clay-heavy sand can cause temporary mesh clogging inside the drum.
  • Bulk Material Size: Oversized stones beyond intake limits require preliminary manual sorting.
