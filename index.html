Project Overview

Introduction

Manual sand sieving is time-consuming and labor-intensive. This Motor-Driven Sand Sieving Machine utilizes a motorized mechanism with a rotary/oscillatory drum sieve to quickly separate fine sand from gravel and debris[cite: 2].


Designed completely in SolidWorks[cite: 1], this project focuses on structural stability[cite: 1, 2], efficient power transmission via V-belts[cite: 2], and robust sheet-metal discharge chutes[cite: 2].

Key Benefits

  • High screening efficiency and output rate[cite: 2].
  • Reduces physical fatigue and manual labor costs[cite: 2].
  • Sturdy frame constructed for durability on construction sites[cite: 1, 2].
  • Smooth belt-pulley power transmission system[cite: 2].

Main Machine Components

Structural Frame

Constructed using strong metal profiles to ensure overall machine stability[cite: 1, 2] and absorb vibrational loads during high-speed sieving operations.

Motor & V-Belt Drive

An electric motor drives the pulley system connected via a durable V-belt[cite: 2], transferring rotational power smoothly to the mechanism[cite: 2].

Sieve Drum

A perforated cylindrical mesh drum[cite: 1, 2] where raw sand is fed, separating coarse gravel from usable fine sand.

Discharge Chute

An inclined sheet-metal chute that collects and guides the sieved sand cleanly out of the machine[cite: 2].
